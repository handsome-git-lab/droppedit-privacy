Dropped It — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Contact: handsomegit3@gmail.com · Handsome Git Lab

The short version

Dropped It: Small Item Finder looks through photos of the ground, a floor or a table for small things that stand out. All of that analysis happens on your phone. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot upload your photos, searches, item names or locations, and it has no account, no ads and no analytics. Anything that leaves the app does so because you chose to share it, open it in another app, or buy Pro through Google Play.

What the app accesses

  • Camera, when you take photos for a search, use the live camera tools (the room sweep, the live spot finder) or the shine check, which also uses the torch. Camera frames are analysed on the phone and not kept unless they become a photo in your search.
  • Photos you choose through Android's photo picker. The app sees only the photos you pick, not your whole gallery.
  • Location, optional. Only if you turn on "Remember this place" or tap "Save this spot", and only while the app is open. It is used to save where a search happened, to show how far away and which way a saved search is on the Places map, and to time a first-light reminder. The app does not use location in the background.
  • Notifications, optional. Used only for reminders you set to come back to a search. Reminders are scheduled on the phone and restored after a restart.
  • Motion and magnetic sensors. The compass turns the arrow on the Places map; the magnetometer drives the experimental metal sensor. Readings are used live and not stored.

The app does not request contacts, microphone, phone, SMS, call logs, files outside its own storage, or advertising-identifier access.

What is kept on your phone

Dropped It keeps these in its private app storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your recent searches (up to 40): the photos used, the item photo, the name you gave the search, the spots found and what you marked, painted searched areas, and, if you saved them, the location and a photo of the surroundings.
  • Item photos you save in "My small things".
  • The setup you are working on in each mode, so it survives the app being closed by Android.
  • Scheduled reminders, your settings (such as the spot colour) and a cached record of whether Pro is unlocked.

The app copies the photos a saved search needs, so the search still opens if you later delete the originals from your gallery; deleting the search removes those copies. Selecting a photo never deletes or changes the original. Help → "Free up space" removes temporary copies no saved search uses. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Backups

Your photos, searches, saved items, the setup in progress, reminders, location setting and Pro status are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Simple preferences such as the spot colour may be included in your Google backup if you have backup turned on.

When something leaves the app

  • Sharing a spot. "Share this spot" makes a close-up and the whole photo and hands them, with a short text, to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. The files are kept in the app's cache for a day so the receiving app can open them. The receiving app or service can keep and distribute what you send; Dropped It cannot delete those copies.
  • Open in Maps. Hands a saved search's coordinates and name to your map app. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Diagnostic report. From Help, you can copy or share a short report of the app version, device model, Android version, screen and memory figures, and counts of saved searches. It contains no photos, file paths, search names or locations, and is sent only if you choose to.
  • Reminder notifications show the search name and a photo of the area on your phone. On a locked screen they show only that a reminder is due.

Google Play purchases

Dropped It Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. The purchase and payment are handled by the Google Play Store app under the Google account on your phone; Dropped It never sees your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Pro is owned and keeps a local yes/no record. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Dropped It is a general utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it collects no personal information at all.

Your choices

You can decline camera, location and notification access and still use the parts of the app that don't need them. You can delete any saved search or all of them (Places → menu → Delete all searches), remove saved items, and turn "Remember this place" off at any time. There is no account to delete.

Changes and contact

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date. Revisions are kept in this website's repository history. Questions: handsomegit3@gmail.com, Handsome Git Lab.
